⚠️ Capacity Tightening Accelerating

  • 🚛

    Class 8 Sales Crisis

    November 2025 was the lowest November retail month since the 2009 financial crisis. 12,479 units sold — down 36.5% Y/Y.

  • 📉

    Carrier Exits Continue

    Major closures: 10 Roads Express (2,000+ workers), Texas International (Chapter 11), Montgomery Transport (Chapter 7).

  • 👤

    CDL Enforcement Impact

    170+ immigrant CDL holders arrested. 194,000-437,000 non-domiciled CDL holders potentially affected (5-12% of all CDL holders).

  • ⛽

    Fuel Price Outlook

    2026 diesel forecast: $3.47/gallon average (-4% Y/Y). EIA projects steady decline through year.
